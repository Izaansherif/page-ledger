# STORY-NOTES — the owner's captions for the build story

The home page has a collapsed section called **"The story of this build —
straight from the commits"**. Its structure is fixed by code:

- **days** = the real `git log`, grouped by date (`js/timeline.json`,
  regenerated by `node scripts/make-timeline.js`).
- **commit subjects** = shown as-is, word for word.
- **captions** = one sentence per day, in YOUR words. The page never
  invents one. A day without a caption shows only its commits.
- **origin box** = two columns ("What existed before the hackathon" /
  "Built during the hackathon"), also filled only with your words.

Where captions live: `js/story-notes.js` (`CAPTIONS` keyed by date,
`STORY_BOXES.before` and `.during`). Paste your sentence under the
matching date and the page picks it up on reload — no other changes.

---

## How to answer

Write one or two sentences each, plain and honest. Plain English is
perfect (judges read English); a word of Hindi/Malayalam is fine when
it is natural for you. Do not polish them for marketing — they read
better exactly as you'd say them to a friend who knows nothing about

- AI
- coding.
