Where your pages live

Right now, everything is on this device and nowhere else.

  • Photos stay on your phone — resized in your browser, kept on this device.
  • Only you can read them — there is no shared place anyone else can look.
  • AI reads, you confirm — unreadable fields stay blank; nothing is kept until you check it.
  • Messages never send themselves — WhatsApp words are templates you review and send.
  • Cloud, when linked — when you connect your own cloud project, pages and photos go only into your private account. No one else can read them.
